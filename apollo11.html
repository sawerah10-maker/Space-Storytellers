Apollo 11 Lunar Module

The equipment that helped humans reach the Moon and remained behind after the historic mission.

THE STORY BEHIND THE EQUIPMENT

Humanity's First Steps on the Moon

Apollo 11 was NASA's historic mission that successfully landed the first humans on the Moon on July 20, 1969. Astronauts Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin descended to the lunar surface using the Lunar Module named Eagle.

What Was the Lunar Module?

The Lunar Module was specially designed to carry astronauts from lunar orbit to the Moon's surface and back. It had two parts: the descent stage and the ascent stage.

Why Was It Left on the Moon?

After completing their work on the lunar surface, Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin climbed into the ascent stage and returned to lunar orbit. The lower descent stage was not needed for the return journey, so it remained on the Moon.

A Piece of Space History

The Apollo 11 descent stage remains at the historic landing site in the Sea of Tranquility. It is a reminder of humanity's first successful journey to the lunar surface.
