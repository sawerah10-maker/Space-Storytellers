MOON'S EXPLORATION HISTORY

The Machines That Remain

Three explorers. Three remarkable stories.

01
1969 · APOLLO 11 MOON LANDING

Apollo 11 Lunar Module Descent Stage

Apollo 11 carried astronauts Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin to the Moon in 1969. After they returned to lunar orbit, the Lunar Module's lower descent stage was left behind on the Moon.

02
1971 · APOLLO 15 MOON ROVER

Lunar Roving Vehicle

NASA's Lunar Roving Vehicle helped astronauts travel across the Moon during Apollo 15, 16 and 17. These electric rovers were left behind on the lunar surface after their missions.

READ THE STORY →
03
1969 · APOLLO SCIENCE EXPERIMENTS

Apollo Lunar Surface Experiments Package

NASA astronauts installed ALSEP instruments on the Moon to study its environment. These experiments collected scientific data about moonquakes, the lunar surface and the space environment.
